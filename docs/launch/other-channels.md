# Other launch channels (#112)

## Lobste.rs
- Requires an invite from an existing member. Ask someone in your network, or a
  friendly maintainer, well before launch week.
- Tags: `rust`, `privacy`, `networking`, `security`.
- Same honesty bar as HN. Submit the repo; put context in the first comment.
- The audience is small but unusually high-signal; expect sharp protocol questions.

## r/opensource
- **Title:** `Lifeline: Apache-2.0 offline mesh messenger — looking for testers and contributors`
- Lead with the open-source angle: license, governance, good-first-issues, how to
  build, how to contribute. This community rewards "come help" over "look at my thing."
- Link `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the `good first issue` label.

## Hacker News (non–Show HN follow-ups)
